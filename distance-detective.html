How to play & how points work

Distance Detective

There's a secret country. Type any country name to guess it — you'll be told how far away (in km) and which direction (N/NE/E/SE/S/SW/W/NW) the real country is from your guess. Use that to narrow in.

  • Rookie — well-known countries, 10 pts, 3 hints available.
  • Explorer — moderately known, 20 pts, 2 hints available.
  • Globetrotter — tricky ones, 30 pts, 2 hints available.

Points start at the tier value and drop slightly with each wrong guess and each hint used, so faster is worth more. In Live Mode, fill in "Guessed by" before submitting the correct country so the win is logged to the right viewer. In Offline Mode, just guess for yourself.

Gifts: unlock a shared boost (narrows the region for everyone) rather than helping one viewer alone.
