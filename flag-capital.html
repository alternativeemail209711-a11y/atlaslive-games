How to play & how points work

Flag → Capital

Only a country's flag is shown — never its name. Figure out the country from the flag, then comment the name of its capital city.

  • Rookie — well-known countries, 10 pts, 3 hints available.
  • Explorer — moderately known, 20 pts, 2 hints available.
  • Globetrotter — tricky ones, 30 pts, 2 hints available.

Each hint you reveal lowers the round's value slightly. In Live Mode, type the winning viewer's name and tap Award. In Offline Mode, type your own guess directly — a correct guess auto-awards your solo score.

Gifts: a gift doesn't hand any one viewer an advantage — it unlocks one shared boost for the whole chat, like an extra letter reveal.

Tap the ⚙ icon any time to change difficulty, mode, or timer length — your choice carries over automatically to every future round until you change it again.
